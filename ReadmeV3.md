## About

I'm a back-end engineer who cares about the last 10% as much as the first 90%. Over **7.5+ years** I've
built for teams that can't afford downtime — telecom billing, ISP network monitoring, ERP and government
platforms processing real money and real infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of **reliability and architecture**. I sweat the details users never see:
query plans, cache invalidation, multitenant boundaries, and codebases that stay maintainable years after
handover.

- 🏗️ Currently **Senior Software Engineer (Team Lead)** at **B-Trac Solutions Ltd.**, Dhaka
- ⚡ Focused on **distributed system design**, **performance optimization at scale**, and leading teams
      that ship enterprise software without compromising on quality
- 🧩 Deep in **C# / .NET Core**, **SQL Server**, **Redis**, **Azure Service Bus** — with **Next.js** and
      **Blazor** on the delivery side
- 🌏 Shipped systems for **BTRC-regulated telecom**, **ISP infrastructure**, and **government** clients
      across Bangladesh and Myanmar
